OUR POINT OF VIEW

Not an antique shop.
A gallery of extraordinary objects.

Olive Vintage Gallery is built around discernment. We look for glass that feels unmistakable: exceptional color, controlled form, unusual technique, strong attribution, compelling age or a maker's distinct hand.

Contemporary, vintage and antique works live together here because quality transcends period.
