OWNER HANDOFF

What the owner does later

  1. Sign in to the private Gallery Manager.
  2. Open Owner Settings → Payments.
  3. Choose Sandbox while testing, or Live when ready.
  4. Paste the Square Application ID, Location ID, and Access Token.
  5. Optionally paste the Webhook Signature Key.
  6. Save, then press Test Connection.
No Square information is required at website delivery.

The next owner can connect or replace the Square account without editing code.
